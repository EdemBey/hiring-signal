What a job board says about a company

Every public posting is a dated signal: what a company is buying, where, at what level, and how well it maintains the pipeline. This reads any public SmartRecruiters board and turns it into one page of diagnostics.

The identifier is the first segment of any job URL: jobs.smartrecruiters.com/Ramboll3/… → Ramboll3. Works on any company with a public board. No key, no login.

What this cannot tell you

  • No hires, no applications. A posting is demand, not outcome. Nothing here says how many people applied or were hired.
  • Posted date is not first-published date. Boards get republished in bulk, so month-on-month volume can move for reasons unrelated to hiring.
  • Duplicate clusters can be legitimate. Several open heads on one role look identical to a repost.
  • One board is not a market. The comparison is against whatever other boards are public on the same ATS — not against an industry average.
  • Country counts are where roles are open, not where the company operates.
Source: SmartRecruiters public posting API · public postings only, no authentication, cached locally, rate-limited.
